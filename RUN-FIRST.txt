FFXIV CLEAN STREAM — QUICK START

FFXIV Clean Stream creates the overlay-free preview that you share in Discord.
Your normal FFXIV window continues to show the MMOMinion GUI.

1. Start FFXIV in DirectX 11 mode.
2. Start MMOMinion and wait until its GUI is visible.
3. Run FfxivCleanStream.exe. If FFXIV runs as administrator, run this
   app as administrator too.
4. Leave 1280 × 720 and 30 FPS selected, then click “Start / Resume”.
5. Wait for the status to say “Ready”.
6. In Discord, share the window named “FFXIV Clean Stream”. Do not share
   FFXIV, your entire screen, or “FFXIV Clean Stream — Standalone Controls”.

The EXE and FfxivCleanStreamHook64.dll must stay together in the same folder.

The preview choices are 1280 × 720 (default) and 1920 × 1080. Both are
16:9. The 720p choice gives Discord a smaller window-capture source, but
Discord still chooses the encoded stream quality and may rescale it. The GPU
capture ring remains at FFXIV's native render resolution.

When you finish, click “End stream” or close the preview with its X button. To
stream again, choose your resolution and FPS, then click “Start / Resume”.
Discord may still point at the closed preview, so select the newly created
“FFXIV Clean Stream” window before going live again.

If MMOMinion changes its graphics hook, fully exit and restart FFXIV. Wait
until the MMOMinion GUI is visible before starting capture again.

If performance is tight, click “End stream”, select 1280 × 720 and 15 FPS,
then click “Start / Resume”.

The preview supplies video only. FFXIV audio may need to be selected or routed
separately in Discord.

See README.md for troubleshooting, safety information, and build instructions.
